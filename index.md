  <h2>Hi / こんにちは！ My name's Charlie, Im a penultimate year Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at Imperial College London, UK. Welcome to my corner of the internet!</h2>
  <p>ONGAKU-CIRCUITS was formed from my two passions - music and electronics. This is reflected in the name - "ongaku" (音楽) in Japanese means "music". Back in the 1970s and 1980s, there was a fantastic period of innovation in audio electronics led by pure interest within some Japanese synthesiser manufacturers. I wanted to pay homage to this era and help to resurrect the same level of excitement in the audio electronics niche in this increasingly digital world.</p>
  
  
  <p>This website aims to be an open-source, educational resource for learning about synthesiser design and other audio electronics. This means you can copy all the circuits and code from this website as you wish.</p>

  <p>The projects on this site range from complete beginner to advanced - whether you've never touched any electronic components before or you're a seasoned veteran there'll be something for you explained in just the correct level of detail! </p>

  <p>One of my promises to you is that nothing on this website is AI generated. Every single schematic or code snippet you see on this site has been built and verified by yours truly. If you want a quick, untested project without understanding what's going on then just ask AI - if you want to genuinely understand what you're building then read on.</p>


  <p> If you notice an issue with this website, or with any of the projects, please use the "I've found a bug" button below to notify me. I try to respond to bug fix requests within 7 days.</p>
